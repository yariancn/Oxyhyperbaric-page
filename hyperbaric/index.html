Frequently Asked Questions

Tap any question to see the answer.

What is a mild hyperbaric oxygen session like?

You can sit or lie down inside a private chamber (select your chamber accordingly). The pressure is raised gently — most people compare it to flying — and you breathe normally for 60 minutes while you relax with meditation, music, or a book. No needles, no medication, no downtime.

What's included in the $49 first-session offer?

One full 60-minute mild hyperbaric chamber session for $49. Includes your first consultation free and full protocol support via our personalized app when you book a package. The $49 is paid at the clinic on arrival.

How many sessions will I need?

The number of sessions varies for each person and the wellness benefit you are looking for. At the beginning, daily sessions are usually recommended. Your $49 first session is the starting point — we will suggest a plan after, with zero pressure to commit that day.

Is mild hyperbaric oxygen safe?

Mild hyperbaric is a low-pressure wellness session and is generally well tolerated. We screen everyone before booking to make sure it is appropriate. If you have a recent surgery, a serious lung condition, or are pregnant, please mention it on the form so we can review.

Will I feel something during the session?

Most people feel a gentle ear pop — like an airplane takeoff — as the pressure changes, then settle in for the session. Experiences vary.

Is hyperbaric oxygen covered by insurance?

For wellness uses, no. We do not bill insurance. Your first session is $49, and we will walk you through protocol pricing at your consultation if you decide to keep going.
