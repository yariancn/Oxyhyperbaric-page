What is this, in plain English?

It's a comfy private chair surrounded by red infrared lights. You sit, lightly pedal with your feet, and watch Netflix for 40 minutes. The lights warm your body and help target fat in the belly, thighs, and arms. No sweat, no soreness, no gym.

Will I lose weight from one session?

One session feels great but real changes need consistency. Most clients who come twice a week see real body composition changes in 4–6 weeks. We weigh and measure you every visit so you can see what's actually moving.

Will I be sore tomorrow?

No. The pedaling is super light. The lights do the work. You'll feel warm and a little tired — never sore. Most people head straight back to work after.

What's included in the $49?

A full body scan ($45), the complete 40-min red light cabin session ($95), and a 1-on-1 plan walkthrough afterwards ($40). $180 in total value, $49 for first-time clients only.

Do I have to sign up for anything afterwards?

No. The $49 is the whole visit. No upsell pressure in the room. You take your scan, take your plan, and leave. We only talk about coming back if you ask.

Is there any downtime?

Zero. You can drive yourself home, return to work, pick up the kids — same as before. We just recommend drinking lots of water afterwards.

Who shouldn't do this?

Don't book if you're pregnant, have a serious heart condition, have a pacemaker or implanted electronic device, are running a fever, or are in active cancer treatment. If you're not sure, call us at (713) 591-3379 first.
