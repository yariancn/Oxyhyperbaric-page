Frequently Asked Questions

Tap any question to see the answer.

What is this, in plain English?

It's a comfortable chair surrounded by red infrared lights. You sit, lightly pedal with your feet, and watch Netflix for 40 minutes. The first visit includes a body composition scan and a plan walkthrough.

What should I expect from one session?

The first visit is designed as a baseline: body composition scan, 40-minute cabin session, and a plan walkthrough. Any follow-up routine depends on the plan recommended at the clinic.

How does it feel afterwards?

The pedaling is light and guided. Most visits fit into a normal day, but individual experiences vary.

What's included in the $49?

A full body composition scan, the complete 40-min red light cabin session, and a 1-on-1 plan walkthrough afterwards — $49 for first-time clients only.

Do I have to sign up for anything afterwards?

No. The $49 is the whole visit. No upsell pressure. You take your scan, take your plan, and leave. We only talk about coming back if you ask.

Is there any downtime?

There is no planned downtime for a standard wellness visit. We recommend drinking water afterwards.

Who shouldn't do this?

Don't book if you're pregnant, have a serious heart condition, have a pacemaker or implanted electronic device, are running a fever, or are in active cancer treatment. If you're not sure, call us at (713) 591-3379 first.
