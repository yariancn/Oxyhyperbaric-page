What is mild hyperbaric oxygen therapy?

Mild Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy (MHBOT) is a wellness & longevity approach where you relax in a pressurized chamber and breathe oxygen at levels above normal atmospheric pressure. By increasing oxygen throughout the body, many clients use sessions to support everyday wellness goals — including helping the body respond to inflammation, improving oxygenation, and supporting mitochondrial function (the energy centers inside your cells).
