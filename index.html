What is mild hyperbaric oxygen therapy?

Mild Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy (MHBOT) is a wellness approach where you breathe oxygen in a pressurized chamber at levels above normal atmospheric pressure. It is used for general wellness and relaxation — not as a substitute for medical treatment.
