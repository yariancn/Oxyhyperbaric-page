Session Instructions

OxyHyperbaric · Mild Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy · Shenandoah & The Woodlands, Texas

Thank you for booking with OxyHyperbaric.

To help you have a safe, comfortable first session, please read the following instructions before your visit:

Important restrictions

  • Substances: Do not consume alcohol or recreational drugs within 8 hours before your session.
  • Tobacco: Do not smoke for 24 hours before or after your session.
  • Flights: Do not fly within 24 hours before or after your session.
  • Health: Do not attend with fever or an active respiratory infection. Clients with a history of pneumothorax, barotrauma, or incompatible electronic implants may not enter the chamber.

Clothing & hygiene

  • Arrive clean — no lotions, perfumes, or heavy creams on skin.
  • Wear comfortable clothing (sweatpants or loose pants recommended).
  • Keep nails clean, short, and free of fresh nail polish.
  • Not allowed inside the chamber: jewelry, watches, keys, hair clips, and electronic devices.
  • Eyes: Wear prescription glasses if needed; remove contact lenses before the session.

About your appointment

  • First visit: Arrive 30 minutes early for intake and chamber orientation.
  • ID: Bring a valid photo ID to your first session.
  • Punctuality: Late arrivals may result in a shortened session.
  • Cancellations: Cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours in advance through our booking portal or by contacting us directly. Late cancellations or no-shows may be forfeited per our Terms & Conditions.
  • Booking required: All sessions are by appointment only.
